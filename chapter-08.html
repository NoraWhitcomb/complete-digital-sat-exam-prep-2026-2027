Question 507

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (C = 22h + 30): the next sentence explains why it is right. The one-time fee of $30 is the starting amount, and $22 per hour multiplies the number of hours. Swapping the 30 and the 22 makes the fee grow with each hour, which reverses their roles. Adding the two numbers into 52h, or subtracting the fee, misreads how the charges combine.

Question 508

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (H = 24 - 1.5t): the next sentence explains why it is right. The candle starts at 24 centimetres and loses 1.5 centimetres each hour, so the height is 24 minus 1.5 times t. Adding 1.5t would make the candle grow taller as it burns. Multiplying 24 by t, or using 22.5t, mixes up the starting height and the rate.

Using 800 as the rate forgets that the table moves in steps of 2 hours. Using 1,300 as the start ignores that it is the value at 2 hours, and 650x does not fit the table.

Question 516

Answer: A

One hour of work costs 65 plus 80, or $145, so the total for one hour is not $65. The fixed charge is $80, and 65 is not a number of hours.

Question 522

Question 523

Answer: A

Question 530

Question 531

Answer: A
