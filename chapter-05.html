Question 240

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (Nair found that clover between wheat rows raised soil organic matter by about 30%): it meets the goal. This choice states the study's main finding, that clover raised soil organic matter by about 30%. Naming Nair's job and home province introduces her but does not give the finding. Explaining what organic matter does, or how many fields were used, adds background rather than the main result.

Question 241

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (However,): it shows the logical link between the sentences. The exhibit was expected to attract children, but adults without children made up over half of visitors, which is a contrast. Therefore would mean the adult visitors were a result of the expectation, which makes no sense. For example and similarly do not fit a result that goes against what was expected.

Question 243

Answer: C

Use a result transition when the second sentence shows an effect.
