The correct answer is B (By arguing that the scans show she reworked her portraits over time): it reflects how the two texts relate. The X-ray scans show faces moved and backgrounds changed, which suggests she reworked her portraits over time. The scans contradict the early accounts, so the second author would not agree with them.
