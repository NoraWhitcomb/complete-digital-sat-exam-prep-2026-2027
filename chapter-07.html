Question 452

Question 457

Answer: C

Question 458

Question 459

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (y = 2x - 5): the next sentence explains why it is right. Parallel lines have the same slope, 2, and the line passes through (0, -5), so y = 2x - 5. Slopes of -2 or 1/2 give lines that are not parallel to line k. The equation y = 2x + 3 is line k itself, not a line through (0, -5).
