Question 635

Answer: B

The correct answer is B ((x + 3)(x + 4)): the next sentence explains why it is right. You need two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7, which are 3 and 4. The pair 2 and 6 multiplies to 12 but adds to 8, and 1 and 12 adds to 13. The choice with two minus signs adds to -7, not 7.

Question 636

Answer: B

The correct answer is B ((x - 6)(x + 4)): the next sentence explains why it is right. You need two numbers that multiply to -24 and add to -2, which are -6 and 4. Swapping the signs gives a middle term of +2x instead of -2x. The pairs -8 and 3 and -12 and 2 multiply to -24 but add to -5 and -10.

Question 637

Answer: D

The correct answer is D ((3x - 5)(3x + 5)): the next sentence explains why it is right. 9x² is the square of 3x and 25 is the square of 5, so this is a difference of squares. Squaring 3x - 5 adds a middle term of -30x. The other two choices do not multiply back to 9x² - 25.

Question 638

Answer: A

The correct answer is A ((x - 5)²): the next sentence explains why it is right. Here, x² - 10x + 25 is a perfect square, equal to (x - 5)². Squaring x + 5 gives a middle term of +10x. The product of x - 5 and x + 5 has no middle term, and the last choice gives -26x.

Question 639

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (4x²(2x - 3)): the next sentence explains why it is right. The greatest common factor of 8x³ and 12x² is 4x², and dividing leaves 2x - 3. Writing 2x - 12 forgets to divide 12 by 4. Taking out only 4x leaves an x behind, and 8x² does not divide evenly into 12x².

Question 640

Answer: D

The correct answer is D ((3x - 2)(x + 4)): the next sentence explains why it is right. Expanding this product gives 3x² + 12x - 2x - 8, which is 3x² + 10x - 8. Swapping the signs gives a middle term of -10x. The other two pairs give middle terms of +2x and -2x.

Question 641

Answer: A

The correct answer is A ((x + 1)(x + 7)): the next sentence explains why it is right. Expanding gives x² + 8x + 7, which factors as (x + 1)(x + 7). Using x - 1 or x - 7 gives a negative constant of -7. The last choice gives a constant of -65.

Question 642

Question 643

Question 645

Question 646

Question 647

Answer: B

Two solutions need a positive discriminant, and a quadratic cannot have infinitely many. The sign of the discriminant tells how many real solutions there are.

Question 659

Question 664
