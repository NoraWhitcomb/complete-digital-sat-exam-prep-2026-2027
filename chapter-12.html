Question 751

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (63): half of base times height gives the area. Half of 14 times 9 is 126 divided by 2, which is 63. The value 126 forgets to halve, and 23 adds the two lengths. The value 46 doubles 23, which also adds the lengths instead of multiplying them.

Question 752

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (120): the width is 8, so the area is 15 times 8. The perimeter of 46 means length plus width is 23, so the width is 23 - 15 = 8. The value 69 adds the perimeter and the length, and 345 multiplies them. The value 225 squares the length, which would only work for a square.

Question 753

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (90π): the volume of a cylinder is π times radius squared times height. Here that is π times 9 times 10, which is 90π. The value 30π forgets to square the radius, and 60π doubles the radius instead. The value 300π multiplies by 30 instead of 9.

Question 754

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (60π): a cone holds one third of the matching cylinder. One third of π times 36 times 5 is 180π divided by 3, which is 60π. The value 180π forgets to divide by 3. The values 30π and 20π use the wrong radius or divide twice.

Question 755

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (27): volume grows by the cube of the scale factor. Tripling every side multiplies the volume by 3 cubed, which is 27. The value 3 grows only one length, and 9 is the change in area. The value 6 doubles the factor, which has no meaning here.

Question 756

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (104): the box has three pairs of matching faces. The faces measure 30, 12, and 10, and twice their sum is 2 times 52, or 104. The value 60 is the volume, and 52 forgets to double the faces. The value 208 doubles the total twice.

Question 757

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (72): average the parallel sides, then multiply by the height. The average of 9 and 15 is 12, and 12 times 6 is 72. The value 144 forgets to halve the sum of the sides. The values 54 and 30 use only one side or add the measures.

Question 758

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (45): the two angles add to 180 degrees. If the smaller angle is x, then x + 3x = 180, so 4x = 180 and x = 45. The value 135 is the larger angle, not the smaller one. The values 60 and 22.5 divide by the wrong number.

Question 759

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (52): complementary angles add to 90 degrees. Subtracting gives 90 - 38 = 52. The value 142 uses 180, which is for supplementary angles. The values 62 and 128 come from subtraction errors.

Question 760

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (20): alternate interior angles are equal. Setting 3x + 10 = 5x - 30 gives 40 = 2x, so x = 20. The value 70 is the angle itself, not x. The values 5 and 10 come from errors in collecting terms.

Question 761

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (1,080): the formula (n - 2) times 180 gives the sum. With 8 sides, (8 - 2) times 180 = 6 times 180 = 1,080 degrees. The value 1,440 multiplies 8 by 180 without subtracting 2. The values 900 and 720 are the sums for 7 and 6 sides.

Question 762

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (108): divide the angle sum by the number of angles. The angles of a pentagon add to 540, and 540 divided by 5 is 108. The value 540 is the total, not one angle, and 72 is an exterior angle. The value 120 belongs to a regular hexagon.

Question 763

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (90): the angles add to 180 degrees. So 6x = 180 and x = 30, which makes the largest angle 3 times 30, or 90. The value 30 is x, the smallest angle. The values 60 and 120 use the wrong multiple of x.

Question 764

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (15): matching sides of similar triangles share one scale factor. The factor is 6/4 = 1.5, so the side matching 10 is 1.5 times 10, or 15. The value 12 adds 2 instead of multiplying. The values 20 and 8 use the wrong factor.

Question 765

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (10.5): the pole and the student form similar triangles. Height over shadow is 1.5/2 for both, so the pole is 14 times 0.75, or 10.5 metres. The value 18.7 flips the ratio. The values 21 and 12 come from other setup errors.

Question 766

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (75): areas scale by the square of the side ratio. The ratio 5/2 squared is 25/4, and 12 times 25/4 is 75. The value 30 uses the side ratio without squaring it. The values 48 and 300 square the wrong number.

Question 767

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (SAS): two sides and the angle between them match this rule. SAS stands for side, angle, side, with the angle between the two sides. SSS needs three pairs of sides, and ASA needs two angles. The AA rule shows similar triangles, not congruent ones.

Question 768

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (4): the small triangle is similar to the large one. The side ratio is 3/9 = 1/3, so the matching side is one third of 12, or 4. The value 9 is the full side of the large triangle. The values 36 and 3 multiply or reuse the wrong numbers.

Question 769

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (They are similar, but they are not necessarily congruent): two matching angles make triangles similar. Equal angles give the same shape, but the triangles can still be different sizes. Matching angles do not make triangles congruent. The triangles must be similar, and similarity does not need any matching sides.

Question 770

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (25): the Pythagorean theorem gives the hypotenuse. Squaring gives 49 + 576 = 625, and the square root of 625 is 25. The value 31 adds the legs, and 17 subtracts them. The value 23 does not fit the theorem.

Question 771

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (15): the Pythagorean theorem gives the missing leg. Subtracting squares gives 289 - 64 = 225, and the square root of 225 is 15. The value 9 subtracts the lengths, and 25 adds them. The value 18 does not fit the theorem.

Question 772

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (5√2): the hypotenuse is a leg times √2. So each leg is 10 divided by √2, which is 5√2. The value 10√2 multiplies instead of dividing. The values 5 and 20 halve or double without using √2.

Question 773

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (7√3): the sides follow the pattern s, s√3, and 2s. The hypotenuse 14 is 2s, so s = 7 and the side opposite 60 degrees is 7√3. The value 7 is the shortest side, and 14√3 doubles too much. The value 7√2 uses the wrong special triangle.

Question 774

Question 775

Answer: B

The correct answer is B (6): the ladder, wall, and ground form a right triangle. Subtracting squares gives 42.25 - 6.25 = 36, and the square root is 6. The value 4 subtracts the lengths. The values 7 and 9 come from adding or other errors.

Question 776

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (8/17): sine is opposite over hypotenuse. The side opposite the angle is 8 and the hypotenuse is 17, so the sine is 8/17. The value 15/17 is the cosine of that angle. The value 8/15 is the tangent, and 17/8 flips the ratio.

Question 777

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (15/8): tangent is opposite over adjacent. For this angle the opposite side is 15 and the adjacent side is 8, so the tangent is 15/8. The value 8/15 is the tangent of the other acute angle. The values 15/17 and 8/17 use the hypotenuse.

Question 778

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (0.6): the sine of one acute angle equals the cosine of the other. The two acute angles add to 90 degrees, so the cosine of the other angle is also 0.6. The value 0.8 is the cosine of the first angle, not the second. The values 0.4 and 0.75 come from subtracting or dividing.

Question 779

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (50 sin 35°): the height is opposite the angle, and the string is the hypotenuse. Since sine is opposite over hypotenuse, the height is 50 times sin 35°. Cosine would give the distance along the ground, not the height. Tangent and division by sine use the wrong sides.

Question 780

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (3π/4): multiply degrees by π/180 to get radians. So 135 times π/180 simplifies to 3π/4. The value 4π/3 flips the fraction. The values 2π/3 and 5π/4 equal 120 and 225 degrees.

Question 781

Answer: A

The correct answer is A (150): multiply radians by 180/π to get degrees. So 5π/6 times 180/π is 5 times 30, which is 150. The value 300 doubles the answer. The values 120 and 108 use the wrong fraction of 180.

Question 782

Question 783

The correct answer is B (25π): area is π times the radius squared. The diameter 10 gives a radius of 5, so the area is 25π. The value 100π uses the diameter as the radius. The values 10π and 20π are circumference values or other slips.

Question 784

Answer: D

The correct answer is D (4π): an arc is a fraction of the circumference. The circumference is 18π, and 80/360 of 18π is 4π. The value 8π doubles the fraction. The values 2π and 18π use half the arc or the whole circle.

Question 785

Answer: C

The correct answer is C (20π): a sector is a fraction of the circle's area. The area is 100π, and 72/360 is one fifth, so the sector is 20π. The value 4π uses the circumference instead of the area. The values 40π and 72π use the wrong fraction.

Question 786

Answer: A

The terms x + 4 and y - 1 give the centre (-4, 1), and the square root of 49 is 7. Flipping the signs gives the wrong centre. Using 49 as the radius forgets to take the square root.

Question 794

Question 795
